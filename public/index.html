04 / a bit about me

Curious about
the whole stack.

I’m studying computer engineering and learning by making things. Some days that means writing software; other days it means getting a microcontroller to behave, or wondering why a circuit is doing something it absolutely should not.

Outside of coursework, I’ve been working on small products, real-time apps, and the occasional photo story. I like the part where a vague idea becomes something another person can actually use.

This site is intentionally a work in progress — much like the person it belongs to.
